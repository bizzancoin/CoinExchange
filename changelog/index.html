09/262026
Deposit channel upgrade: any-amount crediting + multi-layer verification
  • Any-amount crediting: integrates the payment service's new capability — users no longer need to create an order with an amount first; whatever is actually paid on-chain is credited, reducing missed deposits and manual fixes caused by amount mismatches.
  • Multi-layer verification at the funds entry point: a deposit is credited only after passing signature verification, order ownership and receiving-address consistency checks in turn, plus an active second check by the platform with the payment service; the same on-chain transaction is credited only once. The signature algorithm has been upgraded to HMAC-SHA256 — a stronger signature algorithm that further protects funds.
  • All clients in sync: web, H5 and app went live at the same time, with error messages in more than ten languages.

Value: higher deposit conversion without extra operations staff, and "forged deposit" risks blocked at the mechanism level.

Spot orders: manual fill from the admin console and stronger funds checks

New manual fill of limit orders from the admin console: limit orders that have stayed unfilled for a long time can be filled at their order price from the admin console. Fees are charged as usual and the user's record is identical to a normal fill; every action is recorded, tracing who did it and when. The same applies to forex, gold, stocks, indices and other instruments.

In the same round, funds checks for ordering and settlement were strengthened:

  • Order amounts are calculated by the server, no longer taken from the amount submitted by the client, eliminating mismatches between the amount and the actual order size.

  • Frozen funds are released per order precisely, so multiple orders in the same coin don't affect each other's frozen amounts.

Value: long-standing open orders now have a clear way to be handled; and every deduction in the funds flow has its own independent check, so account accuracy is guaranteed by the mechanism rather than relying on callers being correct.

App v1.0.0
First official release

We did not take the "web wrapper" approach. We chose Flutter cross-platform native development and rewrote everything as native screens — from login, markets, charts, ordering and settlement to assets, deposits and withdrawals. Compared with a wrapper, this brings three clear differences: faster startup, responsive interaction, and eligibility for official app stores.

1. Core trading: fully native ​

  • Markets and charts: professional TradingView charts with multiple timeframes and a full set of indicators; prices are pushed in real time, and the quote at the top of the chart updates sub-second.
  • Seconds contracts: order → countdown → automatic settlement, all native; the countdown is based solely on the server's fixing time, so what you see is the real settlement moment.
  • USDT-margined perpetuals: a complete loop of transfer, open, hold and close — market / limit orders, 10-level order book and real-time P&L on one screen.
  • Spot and instant swap: both trading methods side by side, for users with different habits.
  • TradFi (forex / commodities / stocks / indices): the same trading experience as digital assets for traditional instruments; categories and instruments are all configurable in the admin console.

2. Assets and funds ​

  • Asset overview, account breakdown (platform / wealth / contract accounts at a glance), transfers, deposits, withdrawals, deposit/withdrawal history and ledger — all native.
  • The deposit page correctly labels TRC20 / ERC20 / BEP20 and other networks per coin, and shows the "wrong-chain deposits cannot be recovered" warning before the user acts.
  • The asset page shows every coin held, with redundant decimal places removed while keeping precision for small amounts.

3. Wealth products ​

  • Wealth management / staking / assisted lending / mining are all native screens.
  • Token subscription (IPO): project list, subscribing, orders and status changes are fully native.
  • Copy trading / lead trading: follow with one tap using a copy code; lead traders issue codes; the whole process is managed by the system.

4. Account and security ​

  • Sign-up / login / password recovery: the app has full standalone registration, with no jump to the web.
  • KYC, email verification, bank cards, terms, VIP tiers and live chat are all native.
  • HTTPS + HTTP/2 at the network layer for faster responses; expired sessions are detected immediately and redirected safely.

5. Operator-configurable ​

The platform's admin console controls how the app looks, with no new release needed:

  • Bottom navigation tabs, home service grid, "Me" menu and asset page tabs are all driven by admin configuration;
  • Announcements, help center, download center, white paper and referral center content are maintained in the admin console;
  • Referral deep-link attribution: when users install the app from a referral link, the referral relationship is carried over automatically, so the agent/referral system continues fully in the app.

6. Languages ​

7 built-in languages: Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese and Thai. The language list is controlled in the admin console and can be switched on or off per operating region.

08/022026
Trading stability: risk controls for extreme markets

For abnormal scenarios such as extreme markets, network jitter and market data outages, we specifically hardened contract liquidation and seconds-contract settlement.

  • Protection against distorted prices: a quote-freshness check. If the market data feed is interrupted, stale quotes are detected and rejected, and the system pauses liquidation and conditional-order triggers for that instrument until data recovers, then re-evaluates at the real market price. The principle is clear: a delayed liquidation is acceptable; a wrong liquidation is not.
  • Protection against double settlement: user close, take-profit/stop-loss, forced liquidation and manual admin handling all share one position-ownership mechanism, so a position is settled exactly once — no double settlement and no duplicate debits or credits.
  • No hanging orders: if market data is temporarily missing when an option-style order expires, the system uses a "bounded wait + timeout fallback" strategy — it first waits for data to recover, and on timeout cancels the order and refunds the principal to its source with a record. There are never "charged but neither settled nor refunded" hanging orders.
  • Consistent time display: client countdowns are now based solely on the server's expiry time, with clock calibration, fixing the systematic drift between client timing and the real fixing time — what users see is the real settlement moment.
  • Ready to scale out: settlement jobs use a global mutual-exclusion mechanism, laying the groundwork for multi-node scaling without any risk of double settlement.

Value: when dependencies fail, the system now "degrades explicitly, traceably and with idempotent funds operations" instead of "failing silently and running in a broken state". This round of hardening did not change any P&L calculation or business rule — only the behavior boundaries in abnormal scenarios.

July 2026
07/262026
Funds security: comprehensive security audit and hardening

We carried out a comprehensive security audit of the platform covering login and authentication, funds flows, configuration and keys, and completed all hardening.

  • Account access security: closed a risky password-free login path; restored and strengthened Google 2FA in the admin console, eliminating "switch on but not actually checked"; all key configuration APIs of the admin console are now under permission control.
  • Consistent funds bookkeeping: all funds operations — withdrawals, orders, instant swaps, deposit crediting, refunds and manual adjustments — were reworked into atomic transactions, eliminating balance errors and double crediting under high concurrency by design.
  • Tightened permission boundaries: user-side APIs always use the logged-in identity, preventing unauthorized access to or changes of other users' profiles and addresses.
  • Protection against duplicate payouts: idempotency control on payouts such as wealth settlement and campaign rewards, eliminating duplicate payouts and earnings statistic errors.
  • Keys and external connections: removed legacy outbound data channels and hard-coded credentials, moved all production keys to external management, closed publicly exposed operations monitoring entry points and removed weak passwords.
  • Account protection: tightened the image captcha, password-recovery attempt limits and the initial-password policy for registration.

Value: before going into formal operation, the platform completed a systematic security check-up and remediation; the accuracy of funds bookkeeping and protection against account impersonation are guaranteed by mechanisms rather than manual after-the-fact checks.

06/162026
Payments and deposits: a faster, easier deposit experience

Added the TokenPay digital asset deposit channel, running in parallel with the existing channel; the platform can switch between them as needed without either affecting the other.

  • Dedicated receiving address: each user has a stable, dedicated deposit address that does not need to be requested again for repeat deposits.
  • Order-based deposits: entering an amount generates the coin amount due, a payment QR code and a validity countdown — pay exactly what you see, eliminating wrong amounts and wrong chains.
  • Automatic crediting of on-chain receipts: credited automatically based on the amount actually received on-chain, with no manual review — faster arrival and more accurate reconciliation.
  • Fixed network labels on the deposit page: TRC20 / ERC20 / BEP20 and other networks are shown correctly for the selected coin, preventing asset loss from wrong-chain deposits at the source.

Value: deposits move from "manual confirmation" to a "fully automatic closed loop", shortening user wait times and reducing support and finance reconciliation costs, with dual-channel resilience — if one channel fails, switch immediately without interrupting business.
